Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how tonic.fm ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website and services (the "Service"). By using tonic.fm, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who We Are

tonic.fm is a personal music cataloguing website that lets you organize, rate, and share music you love. You can build a catalogue of songs, create tier boards, compare tastes with friends, message other users, and more.

If you have questions about this policy or your data, see the Contact section at the bottom.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, timestamps of actions (e.g., when songs were added), and interactions with the Service.
  • Technical data: Browser type, device information, and approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Local storage: Theme preferences and certain settings are stored in your browser's local storage. You can clear this at any time through your browser settings.

2.3 Information from Third-Party Imports

If you use the playlist import feature, you provide a URL from Spotify, Apple Music, or Tidal. We use this to retrieve track metadata (title, artist, album) so you can add it to your catalogue. We do not access your Spotify, Apple Music, or Tidal accounts or store their credentials.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To store, display, and organize your music catalogue and associated data.
  • To enable social features: friends, messaging, profile sharing, taste comparisons, leaderboards, and feeds.
  • To generate stats, year-in-review summaries, and export files for you.
  • To send you account-related emails (password resets, notifications you opt into).
  • To display stickers and profile customizations to you and your friends.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Performance of a contract: To provide the Service you signed up for (account, catalogue, social features).
  • Consent: For optional features like stickers, photo uploads, and certain notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: To maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve the Service.
  • Legal obligation: To comply with applicable laws.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information in these limited circumstances:

  • With other users: Your username, profile, catalogue (depending on your privacy settings), tier board, shared cards, stickers, and messages are visible to friends or the public as determined by the features you use.
  • Service providers: We may use third-party services (e.g., email delivery, hosting) that process data on our behalf. They are contractually limited from using your data for other purposes.
  • Legal compliance: If required by law, court order, or government request, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of tonic.fm, our users, or others.
  • Business transfer: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, user data may be transferred. You will be notified of any such change.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account at any time, and we will remove your personal data within a reasonable period.
  • Catalogue and content: Kept until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Messages: Retained as long as your account is active unless you delete them.
  • Technical/usage data: Retained for up to 12 months for security and analytics purposes.
  • Local storage data: Stored in your browser until you clear it. We do not control this once it's on your device.

7. Your Rights

7.1 GDPR Rights (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten"): Request deletion of your data.
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data.
  • Data portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (you can also use the built-in Export Backup feature).
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw consent for processing that relied on it, without affecting prior processing.
  • Lodge a complaint: With your local data protection authority.

7.2 CCPA Rights (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know: Request the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect, and how it's used and shared.
  • Delete: Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt-out: We do not sell personal information, so there is no sale to opt out of.
  • Non-discrimination: You will not be penalized for exercising these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

7.3 Other Jurisdictions

Even if you are not in the EEA, UK, or California, you may contact us to access, correct, or delete your data. We will respond to all reasonable requests.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

tonic.fm uses browser local storage to save your theme preferences and keep you signed in. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising cookies. You can clear local storage at any time through your browser settings.

9. Data Security

  • Passwords are stored as cryptographic hashes, not in plain text.
  • We use HTTPS (TLS encryption) for data in transit.
  • Access to user data is limited to what is necessary for the Service to function.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we work to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

tonic.fm is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own (e.g., the United States). Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses for international transfers. By using the Service, you acknowledge that your information may be transferred to and processed in such countries.

12. Third-Party Links and Services

tonic.fm may reference or integrate with third-party services (Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, email providers, hosting platforms). These services have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their data practices. When you import a playlist, the third-party service may log that request according to their own policies.

13. Your Choices

  • Account: You can edit your profile, change your password, or delete your account at any time.
  • Content: You can add, edit, or remove songs, stacks, stickers, people, and messages.
  • Friends: You can turn off friend requests, remove friends, or block users.
  • Export: Use the Export Backup and Export Usage Data features to download your data.
  • Notifications: You can manage notification preferences within the Service.
  • Browser data: Clear local storage via your browser settings.
